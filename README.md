### Privacy Policy

*Last updated: September 2026*

RememberMed is a medication reminder that works **fully offline**. This policy describes
how data is handled.

#### 1. Data the app uses
You enter, on your own device: medication name, dose, route, interval, treatment start and
duration, notes, follow-up dates and the record of each dose. In the nursing module, also
the data of the patients you register — name or initials, bed, age, allergies and notes.

#### 2. Where the data stays
**On your device only.** The app has no server, creates no account, asks for no login and
sends nothing over the internet. There is no advertising and no usage analytics.

Android automatic backup is **turned off** for this app: data is not copied to the cloud nor
transferred to another device.

#### 3. Third-party data (nursing module)
Health data is sensitive personal data (Brazil's Law 13.709/2018 — LGPD — and equivalent
laws elsewhere). Whoever registers a patient is responsible for that processing: legal
basis, informing the data subject where applicable, minimisation and deletion. The
developer **has no access to this data and is not a processor of it**, because it never
leaves the device. Professional confidentiality still applies inside the app.

Recommendation: record initials or the bed number instead of full names.

#### 4. Export
Reports and calendar files (`.ics`) leave the device only when **you** choose to export
them. From then on they are under your control and subject to the rules of wherever you
send them. The "Google Calendar" button opens the browser with the event filled in; nothing
is sent without your confirmation inside Google Calendar.

#### 5. Permissions

| Permission | What for | If you deny it |
|---|---|---|
| Notifications | alert you at each dose | alerts keep showing inside the app |
| Exact alarm | alert on time, not within an approximate window | the app falls back to approximate scheduling |
| Run at startup | reschedule alarms after the phone restarts | — (without it, the schedule would vanish at the first restart) |
| Vibration | vibrate with the alert | alert without vibration |

The app does **not** request internet, location, contacts, camera, microphone, file or
installed-apps access.

#### 6. Deletion
Deleting a medication, protocol or patient removes the data immediately. Uninstalling the
app erases everything that was stored. There is no server copy — so the developer cannot
recover anything either.

#### 7. Children
The app is intended for adults. Minors should use it under the supervision of a responsible
adult.

#### 8. Health notice
RememberMed is a reminder. It does not calculate doses, does not recommend treatment and does
not replace the prescription, professional guidance or institutional protocols.

#### 9. Changes
Changes to this policy are published on this page, with the update date at the top.

### Terms of Use

Welcome to RememberMed. This document explains what the app is, what it is not, and what each side is responsible for. Please read it before accepting.

#### 1. What RememberMed is

A medication reminder. You enter the medication, the dose and the interval between doses; the app builds the schedule from that, alerts you at each dose, records what was taken and shows when the treatment ends. It has two modules: a personal one, for your own treatment or a family member's, and a nursing assistance one, for people looking after several patients.

#### 2. Who develops it

RememberMed is the individual work of a professional trained in Computer Science and Engineering, in Biomedical Science and in civil aviation (pilot). Contact for anything concerning the app: ongsuporteunionmundial@gmail.com.

That background explains the product's choices, and nothing beyond that. Engineering accounts for the rigour in what the app computes — intervals, times, records, and what to do when a dose is given off schedule. Aviation accounts for the procedural discipline: checklists, redundancy and no improvisation in a critical task — the same logic behind the rights of medication administration. Biomedical training accounts for the care with clinical vocabulary and with the limits of what software may assert.

None of it turns the app into health guidance. The training of whoever writes the code does not validate clinical conduct: the prescription does. There is no company, no server and no support team behind the app — it is offered as it stands.

#### 3. What the app does not do — and why

RememberMed does not calculate doses, does not suggest medication, does not propose clinically appropriate times, does not warn about drug interactions and does not interpret any health data. It repeats the information you typed, at the interval you set.

That is a design decision, not a missing feature. An app that calculated or recommended clinical conduct would become software as a medical device, subject to its own regulatory regime (in Brazil, ANVISA resolution RDC 657/2022). RememberMed stays deliberately outside that scope — which is exactly why it must not be used as a source of clinical decisions. The prescription decides the dose and the time.

#### 4. Alarms can fail

Alerts depend on your device's operating system. Battery saving, do-not- disturb, shutdown, missing permissions, a system update or simply clearing the app from memory can delay or prevent an alarm. No reminder app controls this.

Treat RememberMed as support, never as the only safeguard. Where a delayed dose carries risk, keep a second form of control.

#### 5. Use in the nursing module

The nursing module organises times and records what was administered. Under no circumstances does it replace:

- the medical prescription and the schedule validated by the institution;
- bedside checking and verification of the rights of administration (patient, drug, dose, route, time, documentation, education, form and response);
- the patient's medical record and the official systems of the service;
- the protocols and routines of your workplace.

Records kept in the app are personal support notes. They are not an official document, not a medical record, and do not replace signing off on the prescription. Technical responsibility for administering remains entirely with the professional, under the rules of their licensing body.

#### 6. Patient data and confidentiality

When you register a patient you enter another person's personal data — and health data is sensitive personal data under Brazil's Law 13.709/2018 (LGPD) and equivalent legislation elsewhere.

Whoever enters it is responsible for it: having a legal basis to record it, informing the patient where applicable, keeping only what is needed and deleting what is not. The developer has access to none of it and is not a processor of that data, because it never leaves your device. Professional confidentiality applies inside the app too — including who else can reach your phone.

Practical advice: use initials or the bed number instead of full names, and record no more than you need in order to work.

#### 7. Privacy

RememberMed works offline. There is no account, no server, nothing is sent over the internet, and there is no advertising or analytics. Everything you type is stored on your device only.

Reports and calendar files (.ics) leave the device only when you choose to export them — from then on they are under your control and subject to the rules of wherever you send them. Uninstalling the app erases the local data; the developer cannot recover it.

#### 10. Who it is for

For adults. Minors should use it under the supervision of a responsible adult. The nursing module is intended for licensed professionals, within the limits of their legal scope of practice.

### How to delete your data

There is no account and no server copy, so deletion happens on your device:

- **One item:** open the medication, protocol or patient and tap **Delete**.
- **Everything:** uninstall the app, or clear its data under
  *Android Settings → Apps → RememberMed → Storage → Clear data*.
